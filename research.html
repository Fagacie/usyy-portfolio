Academic Direction

Research Interests

I am interested in exploring how Artificial Intelligence and intelligent computational systems can be applied to practical problems, particularly at the intersection of software engineering, data-driven applications, and connected systems.

Artificial Intelligence & ML

Exploring machine learning algorithms and their application in predictive modeling and intelligent decision-making systems.

Intelligent Systems

Designing autonomous systems that can perceive their environment, learn from data, and adapt to changing conditions.

Data Analytics

Extracting actionable insights from large datasets to drive intelligent application behavior and business value.

IoT & Edge Computing

Pushing computational intelligence closer to hardware and sensors to reduce latency in real-time environments.

Backend & Distributed Systems

Building the resilient, scalable infrastructure required to support data-intensive and intelligent software applications.
