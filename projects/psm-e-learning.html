Overview

The PSM E-Learning Management System was developed as my Final Year Project. It is a full-stack educational platform built from the ground up to solve the challenges of managing online learning resources, tracking student progress, and issuing verifiable credentials.

Key Features

Role-Based Access Control

Distinct, secure workflows and dashboards for Students, Instructors, and Administrators, ensuring data isolation and privacy.

Course Management

Instructors can seamlessly upload materials, create assignments, and deploy structured assessments.

Payment Gateway

Paystack integration for processing course enrollment fees.

QR-Verified Certificates

Automated PDF certificate generation upon course completion, with QR codes that let employers verify certificate details.

System Architecture Pipeline

End-to-end data flow mapping user actors, presentation interfaces, servlet business workflows, and transactional persistence with Paystack and QR verification.
